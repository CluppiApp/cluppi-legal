Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Cluppi app ("Cluppi") uses, why, and what choices you have. The person responsible for your data (the "controller") is Yevhen Zubrytskyi, an individual developer based in Ukraine ("we", "us"). Contact: evgzubritskiy@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • Your reminders, saved places, settings and language stay on your device. Your location is never sent to us.
  • You can use Cluppi without an account. If you create one (Sign in with Apple), we store the minimum needed to run groups.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or third-party tracking.
  • You can delete your account and your data in the app: Menu → Settings → Delete account.

2. Data we use, why, and on what basis

a) Data that stays on your device. Your reminders, saved places (name and address), notification and language settings, profile photo and the history of your reminders. We do not receive this data. Basis: it is processed on your device only, to provide the app you asked for.

b) Account data. When you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a unique account ID, the name you share and your email address, or Apple's private relay address if you hide your email. We use it to create and secure your account, to show your name to the people in your groups (a nickname someone gives you is seen only by that person), and to contact you about the service. Basis: performing our agreement with you (Terms of Use).

c) Group data. Group names, the display names and photos of members, which groups you belong to, and the reminders members send to each other (text, time, status). We use it to deliver reminders to the right people. It is visible only to the members of the group. An invite link contains a random code: anyone who has the link can join the group until you reset it in the group. Basis: performing our agreement with you.

c2) Reports and blocks. If you report someone or block them, we keep the report (who reported, who and what was reported, the reason and your note) and the block. We use them to review the report, protect people and prevent abuse. Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

d) Notification status. Whether your notifications for reminders from other people are on or off, so that a person who sends you a reminder can be warned that it may not reach you. Basis: our legitimate interest in making the service work as people expect.

d2) Push notifications. So that a reminder from a person in your group reaches you even when Cluppi is closed, your phone gives us its push token (a random address Apple issues for this app on this phone) and whether it is a test or a release build. We keep it with your account and use it only to send you reminders from your groups through Apple's push service, which receives the text of that reminder and the sender's name to show the notification. The token is deleted when you delete your account, when you are banned, when Apple tells us it no longer works, or when it has not been refreshed for 90 days. Basis: performing our agreement with you.

e) Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. When you have Premium and an account, we keep with your account what Apple confirms about your subscription: the purchase identifier (Apple's original transaction ID), the plan, the date until which it is active, and whether it is a test purchase. We use it so that your Premium works on your devices and in your groups. Basis: performing our agreement with you.

f) Location. If you allow it, your device uses location to notice when you arrive near or leave a place you saved. The check happens on your device; we do not receive your location and do not keep a location history. Basis: your consent, which you give or withdraw in iOS Settings.

g) What you type or say. Your messages are turned into reminders on your device. AI help is available only to users signed in with Apple. When the app cannot understand a message on its own, and only if you allowed it (Menu → Settings → AI help in chat), the text of that one message is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic, PBC (the Claude AI model) to read it. Sent with it: the current date, time and time zone, the app language, the names of your saved places and the names of the people in your groups, so that the reply can point to them. Not sent to Anthropic: your account ID, email or device identifiers, your location or coordinates, your other messages or chats, your photos or contacts. Our server sees your account ID only to apply the daily limit. The text is not stored by us: our server passes it on and keeps only a count of how many such requests you made each day, linked to your account, for no more than three days, to apply the daily limit. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days (up to 2 years only if its safety systems flag it for misuse) and, under its commercial terms, may not use it to train its models. When you speak, your phone turns your voice into text: on supported devices this happens on the device; where it cannot, Apple's speech recognition may process the audio. Cluppi keeps only the text; the recording is thrown away as soon as the text is made. If you choose to send us a message the assistant did not understand, we receive that message by email. That message is written in your own mail app, so you see exactly what is sent; if you leave the line with the app version, iOS version and device model, we receive that too. That email is never sent automatically. Basis: performing our agreement with you; for the AI reading, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Menu → Settings → AI help in chat.

g2) Questions about your phone. When you ask the assistant why a reminder did not arrive, the app reads the status of notifications, location, microphone, speech recognition, background refresh, the time zone and your subscription on your device, to explain what to switch on. This is read on your device only and is not sent to us. Changing the app language or other settings by chat also happens on your device only.

h) Technical data. Our servers may keep short-lived technical logs (for example the time of a request, an error code and the IP address it came from) to keep the service secure and working. Basis: our legitimate interest in security and reliability.

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Who receives data

Only companies that help us run Cluppi and may process data only on our instructions: Apple (Sign in with Apple, purchases and push notifications), our hosting provider, Supabase, which stores account and group data on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Members of your groups see the data described in section 2c. We may disclose data if the law or a court order requires it.

Anthropic, PBC (USA) receives the text of a message the app could not understand on its own, only from users signed in with Apple who allowed AI help, to understand the request and return the result.

4. Transfers outside your country

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including outside Ukraine and the European Economic Area. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. How long we keep data

Account and group data are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, we also ask Apple to revoke Cluppi's access to your Sign in with Apple account. Your account and group data are deleted from our systems within 30 days; copies in backups are overwritten within 90 days. Push tokens that have not been refreshed for 90 days are deleted. Technical logs are kept for up to 30 days. We may keep data longer only where the law requires it. Data on your device is removed when you delete your account in the app or delete the app.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect what was done before). To use a right, write to evgzubritskiy@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Ukraine, the Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (the Ombudsman); in the EU or the UK, your local authority.

7. Children

Cluppi is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country, if higher), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including encrypted connections. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
