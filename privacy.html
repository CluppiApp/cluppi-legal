Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Pingy app ("Pingy") uses, why, and what choices you have. The person responsible for your data (the "controller") is Yevhen, an individual developer based in Ukraine ("we", "us"). Contact: evgzubritskiy@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • Your reminders, saved places, settings and language stay on your device. Your location is never sent to us.
  • If you create an account (Sign in with Apple), we store the minimum needed to run groups.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or third-party tracking.
  • You can delete your account and your data in the app: Settings → Delete account.

2. Data we use, why, and on what basis

a) Data that stays on your device. Your reminders, saved places (name and address), notification and language settings, profile photo and the history of your reminders. We do not receive this data. Basis: it is processed on your device only, to provide the app you asked for.

b) Account data. When you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a unique account ID, the name you share and your email address, or Apple's private relay address if you hide your email. We use it to create and secure your account, to show your name (or the nickname another person chose for you) to the people in your groups, and to contact you about the service. Basis: performing our agreement with you (Terms of Use).

c) Group data. Group names, the display names and photos of members, which groups you belong to, and the reminders members send to each other (text, time, status). We use it to deliver reminders to the right people. It is visible only to the members of the group. Basis: performing our agreement with you.

d) Notification status. Whether your notifications for reminders from other people are on or off, so that a person who sends you a reminder can be warned that it may not reach you. Basis: our legitimate interest in making the service work as people expect.

e) Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We receive only whether you have an active subscription and until when, not your payment details. Basis: performing our agreement with you.

f) Location. If you allow it, your device uses location to notice when you arrive near or leave a place you saved. The check happens on your device; we do not receive your location and do not keep a location history. Basis: your consent, which you give or withdraw in iOS Settings.

g) What you type or say. Your messages are turned into reminders on your device.

h) Technical data. Our servers may keep short-lived technical logs (for example the time of a request and an error code) to keep the service secure and working. Basis: our legitimate interest in security and reliability.

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Who receives data

Only companies that help us run Pingy and may process data only on our instructions: Apple (Sign in with Apple, purchases and push notifications), our hosting provider that stores account and group data,. Members of your groups see the data described in section 2c. We may disclose data if the law or a court order requires it.

4. Transfers outside your country

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including outside Ukraine and the European Economic Area. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. How long we keep data

Account and group data are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account and group data from our systems within 30 days; copies in backups are overwritten within 90 days. Technical logs are kept for up to 30 days. We may keep data longer only where the law requires it. Data on your device is removed when you delete your account in the app or delete the app.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect what was done before). To use a right, write to evgzubritskiy@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Ukraine, the Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (the Ombudsman); in the EU or the UK, your local authority.

7. Children

Pingy is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country, if higher), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including encrypted connections. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
