Pingy Support

Write to evgzubritskiy@gmail.com. Please tell us what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you use. We read every message and answer as soon as we can.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications are on for Pingy in iOS Settings, that no Focus mode is hiding them, and that Settings → Notification settings inside Pingy has the switches on.

How do I delete my account?

In Pingy open Settings → Delete account. This does not cancel a subscription: cancel it first in your Apple ID settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pingy Premium. You can also use apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

I paid but Premium is off

Open the Subscription page in Pingy and tap Restore purchases.
